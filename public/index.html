BEFORE YOU LACE UP

GOOD
QUESTIONS.

Do I have to be a fast runner?

No. The Social Miles format is designed around conversation and a relaxed effort. Check a session’s confirmed pace and distance before deciding if it’s right for you.

Can I come on my own?

Absolutely. You don’t need to bring a crew to find one. Once meetups begin, arrive early and introduce yourself to the run lead.

Where and when do we meet?

We’re confirming our first meeting points and schedule. Published sessions will list the exact location, time and route. Please wait for a confirmed event before travelling.

Is there a membership fee?

Attendance is free. Giving is optional, and any donation supports the event’s stated cause. Everyone is welcome, whether or not they donate.

What should I bring?

Comfortable running shoes, drinking water and layers suited to the weather. Bring a charged phone, and check any additional requirements on the event listing.

URC / YOUR PRIVACY

KEEPING IT SIMPLE.

This site has no advertising trackers or third-party analytics. Fonts and reference photography are served locally. Published community photos use URC’s media storage.

If you save a first-run plan, only your chosen run format is stored in this browser. It isn’t sent to URC or anyone else. No name, email address or health information is collected by the pace finder.

When event registration is connected, we use your name and selected activity to organise the run. Email and phone are optional and used for event updates. Your pass link is private. Donation records are separate and visible only to authorised team members. Contact a run organiser to request correction or deletion of your details.

External registration or social links, when added, are covered by their own privacy policies.
