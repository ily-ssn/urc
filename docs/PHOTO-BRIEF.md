# URC photograph brief · 28 October 2026

The new pages deliberately reuse the club's existing organiser photographs. These are placement counts, not a claim that the club has supplied this many different pictures. The Baztan screenshots and photographs are references only and are not included as URC media.

For a separate image in every current placement, send **21 image exports: 8 horizontal and 13 vertical**. This consists of **19 photographs plus horizontal and vertical exports of the next event's poster**. It is also fine to reuse a strong photograph in several placements.

Portraits should ideally be 1500px on the short edge. Landscape and portrait crops can be made from a sufficiently large original. Send originals without screenshots or app overlays. Keep the subject away from the extreme edges so mobile crops work. File names matching this list will make placement straightforward.

Gallery albums remain separate: send as many event photographs as you want included, grouped by run, with photographer credits and any download restrictions. The five existing organiser media photographs remain in the current community album until replaced or supplemented.

## Brand files still required

The approved logos are the files supplied by the owner in chat, including the circular R/URC artwork and the COMMUNITY wordmark. The obsolete Eventbrite emblem must not be used. The inline chat images have no downloadable file reference in the workspace; upload the actual PNG/SVG files in a ZIP so the exact artwork can be installed. Do not approximate the marks with a font or silently regenerate them. The entrance remains inactive until the approved downloadable artwork is present.

## Future footage

The homepage recap currently opens actual photographs. A play button/audio player should only be added once the club provides a real video/audio URL and permission to use it. No race footage or Rico Music sponsorship is invented.
